Alarm Frequency Experimental

Counts alarm episodes (each continuous active stretch of one specific Zabbix/VRM alarm), not the flap-style "transitions" the two Flap Report lists above count — a unit with one long-running alarm and a unit that's flapped between healthy and unhealthy six times can both show up here, for very different reasons. Not a live fleet sweep either: this reads the alarm-history database the Power & Infra Alerts poll already keeps, so Refresh re-queries that history rather than re-checking Zabbix/VRM.

  • Click Refresh to load alarm frequency...
